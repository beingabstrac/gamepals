Privacy

Game Pals · last updated 18 September 2026

  • There is no account, and nothing to sign in to.
  • Your games, scores and settings are kept on your device and stay there.
  • The app never asks for your microphone, camera, location, contacts or files.
  • There are no ads and no analytics in the app today.

What is kept on your device

Everything the app remembers is stored on the device you are playing on, and it never leaves it:

  • Sound and vibration settings.
  • Which games you have opened before, so the one-line tip only shows the first time.
  • How you last set up each table: who was sitting where, and which level you picked.
  • The running score between the same players at a game, and your best Classic Snake score.

In the phone and tablet apps this lives in the device's own app storage; on the web it lives in your browser. Clearing the app's data, or your browser's data for this site, erases all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

What the app never asks for

Microphone, camera, location, contacts, calendar, photos, files, health data, or the advertising identifier. The app does not ask for these and cannot use them. Nothing you type or tap is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it: the app makes no network requests of its own.

Playing on the web

The web version is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it records ordinary request information such as your IP address and browser when it sends you the page. That is GitHub's handling, not ours, and it is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive it, and there is no analytics script on the page. The fonts are part of the download, not fetched from anyone else.

Children

The games suit all ages. Because no personal information is collected at all, there is nothing to treat differently for a child: there is no profile, no identifier, and no message anyone can send you.

What changes when ads and online play arrive

Ads and online play with friends are planned, and neither is in the app yet. They cannot be added quietly: an ad needs an advertising identifier and a consent choice, and online play needs an account and a name other players can see. When either is close, this page will say exactly what is collected, what it is used for and how to turn it off, before the version that does it goes out.
