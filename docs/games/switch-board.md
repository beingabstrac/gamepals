# Switch Board

Status: brief (2026-09-24). From the catalog's chill toys (docs/12 F, "Buttons & switches board"). Fidget boards are generic toys.

## The real thing
A busy board or fidget panel: switches, sliders, dials and buttons, there only to be flicked and turned. The pleasure is the click: each control feels and sounds different.

## What makes it feel right
1. Each control clicks its own way: a snap, a notchy slide, a ratchet, a squishy button, a clunk.
2. Something lights up, so each flick shows.
3. No goal, no clock.

## Our design
- Four toggles, three sliders (eleven notches), two dials (twelve notches), four push buttons and one big lever, chunky and candy colored with the app's toy lip.
- A lamp for each control along the top: a toggle, slider or dial that is on, a button pressed an odd number of times, the lever up.
- **Turn everything all the way on and the lamps go rainbow** (a little surprise, mentioned only as "see what the lamps do").
- Tap, drag and turn; keyboard: 1 to 4 flick the switches, 5 to 8 press the buttons, Z, X and C step the sliders, V and B step the dials, L pulls the lever, Enter is done.
- **Done when you say so**, like the other chill toys, with a count of clicks.

## Tests
Every control clicks (toggle, slider, dial, button count, lever); a lamp per control on, all on goes rainbow; a slider or dial set to where it is, or past its end, is not a move; done finishes it and the referee replays the board.
