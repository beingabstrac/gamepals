# Pond

Status: brief (2026-09-25). From the catalog's chill toys (docs/12 F, "Pond with lotus"). Built as M39a.

## The real thing
A koi pond: fish that drift and turn, that dart away when something hits the water and crowd to food; lily pads that bob on the ripples; lotus flowers that open slowly. Calm-toy apps (antistress collections, koi pond live wallpapers) all lean on the same three things: rings spreading where you touch, fish that notice you, and something that changes because you were there.

## Why it is its own toy
Zen Garden is raking sand into lines; Sand Fall is pouring; Clean It is rubbing grime away. This one is living water: things move on their own and answer you, and nothing you do is undone or wiped.

## Our design
- A pond seen from above, a rounded sky-blue blob in a mint garden. Five lily pads, each with a closed pink bud, laid out from the seed.
- **Tap the water:** rings spread out from the spot and fade; pads nearby bob; fish close by dart away and then drift back.
- **Hold the water:** food scatters where you hold, and the koi turn and swim over to nibble it.
- **Tap a bud:** it opens into a lotus, petals springing out one by one.
- Every lotus open, it is done ("The pond is in bloom"). No clock, nothing to lose.
- Keyboard: 1 to 5 open a bud, F scatters food in the middle, Space drops a ripple.
- Rules keep the pads, which are open and how many times food was scattered; the fish and ripples are the scene's, like the raking in Zen Garden.

## Tests
Pads are laid inside the pond and apart; opening a bud twice or a pad that is not there is not a move; food can be scattered any time; every lotus open finishes it; the referee replays test play.
