# Newton's Cradle

Status: brief (2026-09-24). A chill toy (docs/12 shelf F): the executive desk toy of five steel balls on strings (Simon Prebble named and sold it in 1967; the device is older and the name generic). Pull one out, let go, and one flies out the other side; pull two and two fly out.

## What makes it feel right
1. The click as they meet.
2. Two in, two out: the row passing the push along.
3. The swing dying down, lower each time.

## Our design
- **Five balls on strings** in a candy frame. **Drag a ball out**: grab the second ball and the first comes with it, and so on, up to four.
- **Let go**: the whole row swings as one pendulum. On the side you pulled, the pulled group shows the swing; through the middle it clicks and the same number of balls on the far side take it up. Each crossing a click (a softer one as it dies down) and a little lower, until it rests.
- **Done** puts it down; nothing to win or lose. The rules count the swings.
- **Keyboard**: 1 to 4 let that many go from the left, 6 to 9 one to four from the right, D is done.

## Tests
One to four balls may be let go from either side, never all five; swings are counted with the last remembered; done puts it down and nothing moves after; test play finishes. Invariant, every move: each swing adds exactly one to the count, and only done ends it.
