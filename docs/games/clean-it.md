# Clean It

Status: brief (2026-09-24). From the catalog's chill toys (docs/12 F, "Clean the window / coin / rug"). Cleaning toys are a popular generic genre of satisfying games.

## The genre
Something dirty; you rub it with your finger and the dirt comes away, slowly on thick patches, to show what is underneath. It ends with a shine. The pleasure is watching it come clean.

## Our design
- Three things to clean, chosen as levels: **a window** onto a sunny day (grey smudges), **an old coin** with a star (green tarnish), **a stripy rug** (brown mud). All drawn by us.
- The dirt is a few round blotches, up to three coats thick in their middles, from the seed.
- **Rub**: each spot your finger crosses takes one coat off it and the spots beside it. A blue cloth follows your finger. Keyboard: arrows move a ring, Enter rubs.
- The status line shows how clean it is; the last speck gone, stars sparkle and it is done. Nothing to lose, no clock.

## Tests
It starts grubby (up to three coats, never clean); a wipe takes one coat off the spot and the four beside it, not past the edge; a wipe over a clean patch is not a move; the last speck gone it is done, for all three things, and the referee replays the cleaning.
