# Truth or Dare

Status: brief (2026-09-24). Rules: the party game of turns (docs/12 shelf E), in its family-safe form: on your go, choose to answer a truth or to do a dare. Nobody's product.

## Family-safe, and what that rules out
Every card is written by us. No dare is risky (nothing about heights, food challenges, going outside, or touching anyone else), none is unkind about anyone, and no truth asks for anyone's details or secrets that could hurt. They are silly: talk like a pirate, walk like a penguin, the silliest thing you were ever scared of. Pass is always allowed.

## Our design
- **Two to eight people on one phone** (the party table), three goes each.
- **On your go**: two big buttons, Truth or Dare. The card springs up; read it out and do it.
- **Done** scores a point; **Pass** scores nothing and moves on, no questions asked.
- **Forty truths and forty dares**, none twice in a game.
- **The most done after three goes each wins**; all level is a draw. It is there to give the evening an end, not to make anyone do what they do not want to.
- **Keyboard**: T or D picks, Enter is done, P passes.

## Tests
Pick, get a card, do it or pass, and the phone goes on; no card twice in a game; three goes each then the most done wins, all level a draw; every card its own with plenty of both; test play finishes; the same seed deals the same cards. Invariant, every move of random games: only a done ever scores, and only for the player whose go it is.
