# 2048

Status: building.

## The real game
- 4×4 grid. Swipe up/down/left/right: **every tile slides as far as it can**; two tiles with the same number that collide **merge into one with double the value** — each tile merges **at most once per move**. Score increases by the value of each new merged tile.
- After every move that changes the board, a new tile appears on a random empty cell: **2 with 90% probability, 4 with 10%**. A move that changes nothing isn't a move.
- Reach the **2048** tile to win (you may keep going); the game ends when no move can change the board.
- Created by Gabriele Cirulli (2014), open source under the MIT license — the rules are free to use; we use our own look and code.
- Sources: [Wikipedia – 2048](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2048_(video_game)) · [Math behind 2048](https://dinogame.gg/blog/the-math-behind-2048/)

## What makes it feel right
1. **Smooth slides** — tiles glide, they don't teleport.
2. The **merge pop**: the new tile swells and settles.
3. New tiles **spring in**, so you always see what appeared.
4. Colors that step up as numbers grow — you read the board at a glance.
5. "One more game": instant restart, best score to beat.

## Our design
- **View:** a soft white board with rounded wells; tiles are flat candy colors that climb the palette (cream → peach → tomato → bubblegum → grape → sky → mint → sunny → ink for 2048), crisp Fredoka numbers.
- **Controls:** swipe anywhere on the board (arrow keys on desktop).
- **Engine:** pure rules with tile identities, so every tile's move, merge and spawn is known and animated; seeded spawns for replays and **Race mode** (same seed for two players) later.
- **Solo:** one chair at the table; score shown live; result says the score and whether 2048 was reached.
- **Bots:** a helper for the future "watch / hint" feature — Pip moves randomly, Nova thinks two moves ahead.
- **Different from our other games:** solo number puzzle about planning space, no opponent.

## Tests
Slides and single merges (`2 2 2 2 → 4 4`, `4 4 8 → 8 8`), no-op moves are illegal, spawn ratio ≈ 90/10, score, game over on a locked board, replay determinism.
