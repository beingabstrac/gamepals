# Wind Chimes

Status: brief (2026-09-25). From the catalog's chill toys (docs/12 F, "Bamboo chime"). Built as M39b.

## The real thing
A bamboo wind chime: hollow tubes of different lengths hung on strings from a bar, the longest lowest. Wind swings them and they knock together with a soft hollow note. Tuned chimes use a pentatonic scale so any order of notes sounds pleasant; a tube's sound is its note plus an inharmonic overtone (about 2.76 times the note for a free tube), which is what makes it sound like a tube and not a piano.

## Why it is its own toy
Echo plays notes you must copy in order; Newton's Cradle is steel balls passing one knock along a line. Wind Chimes has no order and no line: each tube is its own pendulum with its own swing, and the music comes from them bumping into each other.

## Our design
- Seven candy bamboo tubes, C D E G A C D (C major pentatonic), longest and lowest on the left, hanging from a peach bar.
- **Tap** a tube to push it; **swipe** across them to brush each one you pass, the way your finger went; **Wind** blows a gust through them for a moment.
- Each tube swings as a pendulum (its string plus half its length) with a little damping. Neighbours that meet trade speeds with a little lost and both ring; each ring flashes the tube white.
- The note is synthesized (the note and the 2.76 overtone), like the rest of our sounds, so there is no audio file.
- A little star appears under each tube the first time it rings. Every tube rung and it is done ("Every chime rang"); it can still be played while the sheet is up.
- Keyboard: 1 to 7 push a tube, W or Space blows the wind.
- Rules keep only which tubes have rung, like Switch Board keeps its switches.

## Tests
Each tube counts once; a tube that is not there is not a move; every tube rung finishes it; the referee replays test play.
