# Pop It

Status: brief (2026-09-24). A chill toy (docs/12 shelf F): the silicone push-pop fidget toy, a sheet of bubbles you press down with a pop and then flip over to press again. The fidget toy is a generic object; "Pop It" is the everyday name for it, and the toy is sold by many makers.

## One popping toy, not two
The catalog lists "Pop bubbles" and "Bubble wrap". Both are "press a bubble and it pops", and shipping both would be a reskin. This one keeps what makes the silicone toy its own thing: rainbow rows, shapes, and the flip, where every bubble comes back up on the other side. Bubble wrap folds into it.

## The real toy
- **A squashy sheet in a shape**, bubbles in rainbow rows.
- **Press a bubble and it pops down.** Run a finger along a row and they go one after another.
- **Flip it over** and every bubble is up again.

## Our design
- **Four shapes** at the table: heart, star, circle, square.
- **Domes with a highlight** in rainbow rows; a pressed bubble becomes a dip that wobbles for a moment. The far side's rainbow runs the other way round.
- **Tap or drag** to pop; **Flip it over** appears when they are all down, and the sheet squeezes flat and opens again.
- **Done when both sides are popped**, with the count of pops. No clock, no score, nothing to lose.
- **Keyboard**: arrows move, Space pops, F flips.

## Tests
Every shape has bubbles, none twice; press all, flip, press all again and the sheet is done; a bubble cannot be pressed twice and there is no flip until all are down; the seed picks the shape; test play finishes. Invariant, every move: a press pushes exactly one bubble down, and a flip brings them all up.
