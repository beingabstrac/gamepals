# Would You Rather

Status: brief (2026-09-24). Rules: the old party question game (docs/12 shelf E): "would you rather...?" with two choices. Nobody's product.

## The real game
- **Someone asks a question with two choices**, and everyone says which they would rather. The fun is the split and the arguing about it.
- There is usually no score.

## Our design
- **Two to eight people on one phone** (the party table).
- **Pick in secret**: a cover passes the phone to each player in turn; a tap shows the question as two big cards; a tap on one hides it for the next person. Nobody copies.
- **The split**: both choices with the faces of who picked each, and "+1" on the side most of the table took. A tie is "a dead heat" and scores nobody.
- **A light score so there is an ending**: a point for siding with most of the table; after ten questions the most points wins. All level is a draw.
- **Sixty questions of our own, family-safe**, ten dealt per game, none twice.
- **Keyboard**: Enter looks, 1 or 2 picks, Enter again moves on.

## Tests
The phone goes round then the split is shown; siding with the table scores and a tie scores nobody; ten questions none twice, then the most points wins; all level is a draw; every question is two different things and none repeat; the same seed asks the same; test play finishes. Invariant, every move of random games: nobody scores more than one a round.
