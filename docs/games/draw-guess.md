# Draw & Guess

Status: brief (2026-09-24). Rules: the drawing party game, the family of Pictionary (a trademark, and a product) and of drawing games people have played on paper for ever: one person draws a secret word, no letters or numbers, while the others shout guesses. Ours is Draw & Guess, with our own words.

## The real game
- **One person draws, everyone else guesses** out loud, as many guesses as they like.
- **No letters, numbers or talking** from the one drawing.
- **A time limit**, usually a sand timer of a minute or so.
- **The guesser who gets it scores, and so does the drawer** (in the team versions the team scores). Everyone takes a turn drawing.

## What makes it feel right
1. Seeing the word and thinking "how on earth do I draw that".
2. The shouting while the picture takes shape.
3. The moment somebody gets it.
4. Laughing at the drawings.

## How the best ones do it
The phone versions give a big white canvas, a handful of colors and an eraser, a clock, and a word only the drawer sees. Pass-the-phone ones hide the word behind a cover so the phone can go round.

## Our design
- **Three to eight people on one phone**, on the party table, one or two goes each.
- **The cover** passes the phone to whoever draws next and says who got the last word. A tap shows the word (only you look), another hides it and the drawing starts.
- **Drawing**: a big card, five inks and Clear, lines drawn with a round pen. **Peek** shows the word again for as long as you hold it.
- **Eighty seconds a drawing**, the clock turning red for the last ten. When it runs out nobody scores.
- **Got it!** asks who called it, one tap on their name, and both of you score, with a burst of confetti. "Not yet" takes you back to drawing.
- **140 words of our own** that can be drawn: things in the sky, buildings, animals, vehicles, food, things at home, clothes, make-believe, places, faces, gadgets and nature. No word twice in a game.
- **Keyboard**: arrows move a pen, Space puts it down or lifts it, 1 to 5 pick an ink, C clears, W peeks, Enter is Got it, then number keys pick who.
- **No bots.** Nobody wants a bot's drawing. The test mode plays it as a table where the next person round gets it, and one in three is missed.

## Tests
Everyone draws in seat order and only the drawer moves; a guess scores the guesser and the drawer, time up scores nobody; the drawer cannot guess their own; most points wins, all level is a draw; two goes each goes round twice and the cover knows the last word; no word twice in a game; the same seed deals the same words. Invariant, every move of random games: every drawing adds nought or two points and the drawer never guesses.
