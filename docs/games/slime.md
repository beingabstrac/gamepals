# Slime

Status: brief (2026-09-25). From the catalog's chill toys (docs/12 F, "Slime"). Built as M39c.

## The real thing
Toy slime (glue, activator, color) is a craft and a fidget: you choose its color, mix in glitter, beads, foam balls or confetti, and then the play is squishing it, poking dents in it and pulling it long so it droops and snaps back. Slime-simulator apps are among the most downloaded antistress toys, and they all do the same two halves: make it, then play with it.

## Why it is its own toy
Every other chill toy here is rigid (switches, frames, balls, sand grains, tubes). Slime is the one soft thing: it changes shape under your finger and wobbles back.

## Our design
- A blob of slime sitting on a table, drawn from a ring of 28 points. Each point is pulled back to the rest shape (a little squashed, as slime settles) and to its two neighbours by springs, with damping; the rest shape follows the middle a little, so the whole slime can be tugged.
- **Poke** it: the edge near your finger is pushed in and springs back. **Pull** it: the nearest point follows your finger and its neighbours stretch after it; let go and it wobbles home.
- Seven colors (tap one) and four mix-ins (Glitter, Beads, Foam, Stars) that go in once each and ride along inside, each bit fixed to a point of the ring and a fraction of the way out, so they stretch with it.
- Done when you say, like Sand Fall.
- Keyboard: 1 to 7 color, G, B, F and S mix in, Space pokes, D is done.
- Rules keep the color, the mix-ins and the pokes; the soft body is the scene's.

## Tests
Colors change (not to the same one), each mix-in goes in once, pokes count, nothing after done; test play mixes everything in and the referee replays it.
