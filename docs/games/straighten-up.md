# Straighten Up

Status: brief (2026-09-24). From the catalog's chill toys (docs/12 F, "Straighten pictures"). Tidying toys are a generic satisfying genre.

## The genre
A wall of pictures, all a little crooked. Nudge each one until it hangs level. That is the whole game, and it is oddly calming.

## Our design
- A warm striped wall, frames in candy colors, each with a small picture of our own (sun, tree, heart, boat, cat, flower, mountain, star). Three walls as levels: 3, 5 or 7 frames.
- **Every frame hangs from a nail on two strings** and turns round the nail: push the frame sideways and it swings, as a real one does.
- Every frame starts at least six degrees off. **Within a degree of level it clicks straight** and settles with a little swing.
- A tiny spirit level under each frame: the bubble drifts to the high end and sits in the middle, green, when it is straight.
- Keyboard: Left and Right pick a frame, Up and Down turn it two degrees.
- The whole wall straight and it is done. Nothing to lose.

## Tests
Every frame starts crooked and within reach; a turn to within a degree clicks level, further off stays crooked; turning past the end or to where it is is not a move; the whole wall straight is done, for all three walls, and the referee replays it.
