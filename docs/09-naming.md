# 09 — Naming

## Decision: **Game Pals** (chosen 2026-09-12)
Simple, says the point of the app — playing with people you know (friends, family), plus bots and online pals.

- **iOS title:** `Game Pals: 2 Player Games` (25/30) · **subtitle:** `Offline, Bots & Online Friends` (30/30)
- **Google Play title:** `Game Pals: Offline 2P Games` (27/30) — test variants
- **Tagline:** "Every game. Every way to play."
- **Code/namespace:** `GamePals` (e.g. `GamePals.Rules.Core`), folder `gamepals`.
