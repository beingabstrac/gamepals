## Description (both stores, 4000 limit, 1530 used)

```
Thirty-four games in one app, and every one of them works with no internet.

PLAY IT YOUR WAY
- On your own, against the clock or the board.
- Against a bot, at four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. Bots never cheat. They see exactly what you see.
- Two, three or four of you round one phone or tablet, passing it along.

THE GAMES
Tic-Tac-Toe, Four in a Row, Checkers, Chess, Backgammon, Reversi, Sea Battle, Dots & Boxes, Mancala, Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe, Dominoes, Yatzy, Shut the Box, Solitaire, FreeCell, Spider, Pyramid, TriPeaks, 2048, Sudoku, Memory, Sliding Puzzle, Color Sort, Echo, Classic Snake, Air Hockey, Ping Pong, Tug of War, Reflex Race, Sumo, Penalty Kicks, Snake Battle.

MADE TO BE PLAYED, NOT FOUGHT WITH
- Every game opens with one line telling you what to do first.
- Every game has plain-language rules, with no jargon, before you start.
- Play with a finger, a mouse or the keyboard. Every game works all three ways.
- The app keeps the score between the same players, game after game.
- It fits a phone, a tablet and a big screen, the right way up and on its side.

NO INTERNET NEEDED
On a plane, on the underground, in a car park with one bar: every mode here works offline. Nothing waits on a connection.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO
- No microphone, no camera, no location. Not now, not later.
- No account, no sign-up, nothing to remember.
- No chat boxes for strangers to type into.

Online play with friends and family is on the way. Everything above will keep working without it.```

The keyword field is iOS only; Play reads the description instead, which is why every game is named in it. People search for the game, not for the collection.
