# 06 — Growth & ASO

Goal: organic growth like JindoBlu, plus the social loop he doesn't have (invites, family groups, challenges).

## 1. Store listing
- **iOS title (≤30):** `Game Pals: 2 Player Games` · **subtitle (≤30):** `Offline, Bots & Online Friends`
- **iOS keyword field (100 chars, no spaces after commas):** offline,no wifi,board,chess,ludo,checkers,solitaire,sudoku,family,party,multiplayer,4 player,bot
- **Google Play title (≤30):** `Game Pals: Offline 2P Games` (27) — test variants · **short description (≤80):** "Chess, Ludo, cards & 50+ games. Play offline, vs bots, or online with friends."
- Long description: plain, honest, keyword-rich; list every game by name (people search game names).
- Screenshots: #1 = "Play any game, any way" mode grid; then Ludo, Chess, Air Hockey, family leaderboard, "Works offline ✈️". Localized per market.
- Use Apple Product Page Optimization + custom product pages (one per hero game, e.g. a Ludo page for India ads/links) and Google Play store listing experiments.
- Honesty claims in listing: "No internet needed", "Bots never cheat", "No ads in the middle of your game".

## 2. Keyword strategy
- Don't fight JindoBlu head-on for "offline games" first. Target: **"play with friends"**, **"family games"**, **"2 3 4 player games"**, **"board games offline"**, individual game names (chess offline, ludo offline, checkers two player), **"games with bots"**.
- Track ranks weekly (AppTweak/AppFollow/Astro or similar).

## 3. Owned channels (the JindoBlu funnel)
1. **YouTube channel**: one 30–60s trailer per game + Shorts (satisfying moments, bot Expert beating someone, family game night clips). Every video links to store + web.
2. **Website** `playgamepals.com` or `getgamepals.com` (both free as of 2026-09-12): a page per game — rules, strategy tips, **playable web version** — ranks on Google for "how to play X", "play X online", and funnels to apps.
3. **Cross-promo**: when we have 2+ apps (e.g. a separate Chess-only or Ludo-only app later), cross-link. Inside the app, the game grid itself is the cross-promo.

## 4. Built-in viral loops (our edge)
- **Invite link** to play → friend installs → lands directly in the match (deferred deep link).
- **Challenge link**: "Beat my 2:14 Sudoku" / "Solve this chess puzzle".
- **Family group invites**.
- **Shareable result cards** (image with score + link) for WhatsApp/Instagram stories.
- Async games generate daily return visits ("It's your turn").

## 5. Localization & markets
Launch languages: EN, HI, ES, PT-BR, ID. Market-specific games: Ludo (India, also big in Africa/Middle East), Dominoes (LatAm), Truco-style (Brazil, later). India = volume (low eCPM); US/UK/CA/AU/DE = revenue.

## 6. Soft launch
- Markets: Philippines + Canada (cheap and English-speaking + Tier-1 proxy), then India/Brazil for scale tests.
- Gate to global: D1 ≥ 40%, D7 ≥ 15%, crash-free ≥ 99.5%, rating ≥ 4.6.

## 7. Paid UA
Not needed to start. Later, small tests to find hero game/creative, then let ASO + organic do the work. Apple Search Ads on game-name keywords is the first paid channel to try.

## 8. Update cadence
Every 2 weeks: 1 new game or major variant, "What's New" copy, icon/screenshot tests. Seasonal themes (Diwali Ludo board, Christmas cards).
